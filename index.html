Photon editor
The force between photons is sinusoidal — it alternately attracts and repels with distance, fading out at forceRange. periods is the number of full wave cycles within that range (can be fractional). Opposite and same charges are phase-shifted by half a period, so within the same "layer" one attracts while the other repels. waveOffset (0 to 1) shifts the wave upward: at 0 it's the classic wave (can briefly attract the same charge), at 1 the sign of the force never flips (the same charge always repels). phaseShift is the horizontal counterpart — it slides the wave sideways along distance, in units of one full period (0.5 = shift by half a period).
